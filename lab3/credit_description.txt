Default of Credit Card Clients

Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/350/default+of+credit+card+clients
Original paper: I-C. Yeh, C-H. Lien, "The comparisons of data mining techniques for the predictive accuracy of
probability of default of credit card clients", Expert Systems with Applications 36 (2009), 2473-2480,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2007.12.020
License: CC BY 4.0

Credit card clients of a bank in Taiwan, data from April to September 2005. The task is to predict whether the client
will default on the credit card payment (will not pay the bill) in the next month (October 2005).
The column "default payment next month" was renamed to "default", otherwise the data is unchanged.
30000 rows, 25 columns. The target variable is default. All amounts are in New Taiwan dollars (NT$).

Columns:

ID: client ID
LIMIT_BAL: amount of the given credit (individual and family credit)
SEX: 1 - male, 2 - female
EDUCATION: 1 - graduate school, 2 - university, 3 - high school, 4 - others, 0, 5, 6 - unknown
MARRIAGE: 1 - married, 2 - single, 3 - others, 0 - unknown
AGE: age in years
PAY_0, PAY_2, PAY_3, PAY_4, PAY_5, PAY_6: repayment status in September, August, July, June, May and April 2005
    -2 - no credit card use in that month
    -1 - paid in full
     0 - paid the minimum amount (revolving credit)
     1 - payment delay for one month, 2 - delay for two months, ..., 8 - delay for eight months, 9 - delay for nine
         months or more
    (there is no PAY_1 column, this is the naming in the original data)
BILL_AMT1, ..., BILL_AMT6: amount of the bill statement in September, August, ..., April 2005
PAY_AMT1, ..., PAY_AMT6: amount paid in September, August, ..., April 2005
default: whether the client defaulted on the payment in October 2005 (1) or not (0)
